# Third-party assets

## Smoke Session gestures and props

The embedded `Bundles/rowemod_smoke_session` pack contains nine gestures and
their matching props adapted from **N O B L E's Smoke Session 2.2.1** for reSkate:
https://thunderstore.io/c/reskate/p/NOBLE1337/Smoke_Session/

RoweMod converts the animations for the Streets rider, supplies temporary local
prop attachment and smoke effects, and adjusts camera and bong orientation.
The source pack's audio and object-dropper behavior are not included.

The camcorder is **Camcorder Video Camera** by **James.Hasegawa**, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), modified by NOBLE and
converted for Streets, including geometry normalization and handle alignment:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/camcorder-video-camera-002d346e9faa41b6b8075feced9e0ba7
