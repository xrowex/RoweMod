# RoweMod 3.3.16

Smoke Session's emote props and smoke now appear in the replay editor for newly
recorded performances in the current session.

- Records the camera, beer, standing bong, table bong, and joint prop poses on
  Streets' native timeline, preserving their accepted grip and orientation.
- Reconstructs smoke from recorded puff events, so pausing freezes it and
  scrubbing backward restores the same position, size, and fade.
- Separate emote plays keep separate segments. Props hide at the stop boundary,
  and replay visuals remain separate from the live emote.

## Install and use

Close Streets, replace `BMX Streets/Mods/rowemod.dll` with 3.3.16, then restart.
The in-mod updater also provides this release. The embedded gesture pack needs
no separate download.

Perform a new emote, then open replay while it is still in Streets' recording
buffer. Its props and smoke follow playback and the scrubber automatically.
History belongs to the current session; it is not embedded in saved replay
files and cannot be recovered from older recordings. Multiplayer sync and pack
audio are unchanged.
