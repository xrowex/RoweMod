# RoweMod 3.3.15

Adds Smoke Session's off-bike gestures and props, and fixes custom clothing
failures when reloading an outfit preset.

- Nine gestures share the Emotes browser, with category filters and search.
  Temporary joint, bong, beer, and camcorder props follow the local rider.
  Playing a gesture dismounts first; Stop leaves the rider on foot.
- Camcorder grip and direction, standing and table bong orientations, and
  visible smoke are corrected. Smoke fades briefly after Stop. Props clear on
  cancellation, replay, and scene changes. The pack is excluded from the object
  dropper, and Animation Studio validation clips stay out of the emote list.
- Hidden outfit slots can complete their native equip before becoming hidden
  again, fixing timeouts when the same preset is loaded repeatedly.
- Bundled reference bodies are removed before Streets clones a clothing model,
  fixing extra white body patches protruding through custom clothes.

## Install

Close Streets, replace `BMX Streets/Mods/rowemod.dll` with 3.3.15, then restart.
The Smoke Session pack is embedded; it needs no separate download. Existing
outfit and physics selections are retained. The in-mod updater provides this
release too.

## Verification and limits

Release build and **401 automated checks** passed. All **1,687** compiled game
wrapper references resolve against both tested interop versions; all **16**
native patch signatures match. All **17** native targets have unique ownership
across **201,454 methods in 162 assemblies** for Streets build **26150520** /
**2.0.0.3070.0**. Existing supported profiles and safety checks are retained.

The release DLL completed repeated loads of the local custom outfit preset and
a real map spawn. Hidden slots completed and returned to their saved visibility,
with no inactive-owner coroutine errors or equip timeouts. Renderer inspection
found no leftover reference bodies, and the selected shirt rendered dark in
gameplay. The gestures and corrected props were visually accepted in Streets.

An installed Invisible Eyes bundle contains no renderer or material; this asset
cannot supply a texture, and its slot remains hidden. See
`docs/material-preset-check-20261023.md` for the clothing diagnosis.

Imported gestures and props are local. Multiplayer synchronization, replay
recording, and pack audio are not included. Controller cancellation and
multiplayer appearance have not been independently verified for this release.
