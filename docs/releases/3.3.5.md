# RoweMod 3.3.5

Four-button pie menu, outfit restoration, emotes, and expanded riding and replay tools.

## Menu and character

- Reduced the pie menu to four equal sections: RoweMod at the top, Replay on the right, Emotes at the bottom, and Vehicle Tuning on the left. Removed unused placeholders and matched wheel dividers and highlights to stick selection.
- Added Restore Game Outfit to Rider Appearance. It clears RoweMod model/material overrides and requests the current native outfit through the local character manager, followed by cleanup of unused assets. Saved custom presets remain available.
- Consolidated vehicle tuning in RoweMod while keeping preset controls and the pie-menu shortcut.

## Emotes and media

- Added native emote controls, custom animation discovery, and optional looping.
- Added an independent placed radio with the native boombox model, physics, a volume slider, camera-relative directional audio, and distance falloff. Change dances without restarting the song.
- Added the last five completed songs by video title, cached playback, removal of superseded downloads, and cache eviction when a song leaves the recent list.
- Added replay recording/playback integration for emote props and radio timing.
- Included experimental TV playback and opt-in multiplayer station sharing. Both players need compatible RoweMod builds; each client prepares media locally.

## Riding, effects, and replay

- Added coping-finder, transition, landing-prediction, and related assist controls, with predicted-path and landing-marker visualization.
- Added an option to extend native replay capacity when increasing the physics update rate.
- Raised the replay camera-light maximum to 50,000.
- Included peg-spark visibility fixes and additional replay input/lifecycle diagnostics.

## Testing and known limitations

Release compilation passed with zero errors. The existing suites passed 25 animation checks, 20 native-registration assertions, and 53 tweak/nose-manual guard assertions. Native target and compiled signature audits also passed. These checks do not establish in-game acceptance for every new feature. The four-button layout and native outfit restore need a fresh in-game check. Multiplayer media needs two-client and late-join testing. TV playback remains experimental: a prepared/playing decoder can still report no visible frame, and this release does not claim that issue is resolved. Replay duration and media synchronization also need gameplay verification at the selected update rate.

## Updating

Close BMX Streets and replace `Mods/rowemod.dll` with this release's DLL, or use RoweMod's updater. Keep your existing settings and presets. The radio and TV bundles download when needed; custom emote/animation assets are supplied separately.
